Uptime

Every app you use is a little city of machines and people passing messages. Walk through one, open every building, follow a request from a tap on a phone to a disk and back, and see what breaks when a part goes dark.

  • Tap a building to see what it does, or a person to chat
  • Journeys follow a request; Grow it scales a system up
  • Incidents: get paged, find what broke, fix it

Music plays on entry. Mute it any time with the Music button.
